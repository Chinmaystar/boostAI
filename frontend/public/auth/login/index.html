1A diver steps off a 10 m platform and falls freely. Roughly how fast are they moving when they hit the water? (take g = 9.8 m/s²)
v² = u² + 2as  ·  u = 0, s = 10 m
1v² = 0 + 2 × 9.8 × 10 = 196
2v = √196
Answer  v = 14 m/s
